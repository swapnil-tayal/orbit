Office

ONE PER POPULATED UNIT
  • Station = desk, chair, name tag and a 1 m ring. Pitch 2.2 × 2.0 m.
  • Starts with one row of four, a meeting area and a lounge. Rows of four are added as people join, up to six rows (24 desks).
  • Home is your home unit's office, anchored on your desk. You open the app there.

Shared destination

NO OWNER · NO DESKS
  • One arrival point plus 2–4 gathering circles, open ground between.
  • Circles work like desk rings: step in to join. They belong to no one and are always open.
  • New destinations reuse the template with new scenery. On the globe: a diamond, named only in panels.
