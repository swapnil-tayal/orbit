SPEC · GLOBE

One globe, four heights

Zoom is continuous. What you see changes at four heights. The camera tilts as it drops, so the curve never leaves the screen.

Handling

DragSpins the globe, settles in ~600 ms. At Unit height it pans the surface. Scroll · pinchLog zoom, tilt follows. Hard stop at 50 km. Click a personCamera flies to their unit in 900 ms. A peek: you stay put. Double-clickFocus that unit. Water or desert: "No spaces here". Back to meFlies to wherever your avatar is right now. IdleNo auto-spin in the app. Only the landing drifts.

Privacy by coarseness

Only a unit ID is stored. Search or rough location becomes a unit on the device, then is thrown away.
Markers sit at the unit centre in fixed slots. Never offset from real coordinates.
No labels on the globe. No cities, roads or borders.
Home never follows you. No dragging. Real moves change nothing. Change it only in Profile.
Oceans, deserts, ice, empty land: no homes, no offices, not selectable.

The 50 km grid

Latitude is cut into 50 km bands (0.449°). Each band holds as many equal ~50 km cells as fit, so cells stay near-square. A unit is band:cell. An office exists only where someone lives.
